Who We Are

Empowering Local Businesses,

Many local businesses remain invisible online — customers walk past great shops simply because they don't know they exist. Shopora was built to change that.

We are a Local Business Product Discovery Platform that bridges the gap between neighbourhood shop owners and the customers looking for exactly what they sell. Shop owners get a powerful digital dashboard to list their store, upload products, manage categories, and reach more people — without needing any technical expertise.

Customers get a seamless way to search nearby shops, browse products, compare prices, and decide where to shop — all from one place.
